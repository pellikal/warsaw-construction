# Typefaces

All typefaces are self-hosted (no runtime calls to Google Fonts) and licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits bundling with a commercial website.

- Playfair Display (400) — Claus Eggers Sørensen (OFL 1.1)
- Inter (variable, weight axis; upright) — Rasmus Andersson (OFL 1.1)

Files are the latin subsets packaged by Fontsource (fontsource.org).
